🌎 ABOUT MY TRIP

OMG where do I even START!! This was honestly the BEST trip of my entire life. I spent 9 amazing days in Thailand in March 2003 and I am still not over it lol. Here is a quick breakdown of all the places I visited:

🍹 Bangkok

We started and ended our trip in Bangkok, the incredible capital of Thailand!! This city is INSANE in the best way. We visited the Grand Palace and Wat Phra Kaew (the Temple of the Emerald Buddha) which was absolutely breathtaking — I could not believe how detailed and colorful everything was. We also went to Wat Pho to see the giant reclining Buddha, took a boat along the Chaophraya River, and explored the famous Khao San Road at night. The street food here was some of the best I have ever had in my life — pad Thai, mango sticky rice, tom yum soup... I ate SO much food lol. Bangkok is chaotic and loud and amazing and I loved every second of it.

🏖️ Phuket

From Bangkok we flew south to Phuket, Thailand's largest island!! Phuket Town itself is really cool with all the Sino-Portuguese architecture and the Sunday Walking Street market was incredible. But mostly we were there to use it as a base for beach days and island hopping. Patong Beach was beautiful but a little touristy — we preferred the quieter beaches on the west coast. The sunsets here were absolutely unreal, I have never seen colors like that in my life!!

🏜 Phi Phi Islands

OK so Phi Phi was literally the most beautiful place I have EVER been. We took a speedboat out from Phuket and spent a day exploring Phi Phi Leh (the famous one from The Beach movie!) and Phi Phi Don. The water is this insane turquoise color that looks fake and there are limestone cliffs everywhere. We went snorkeling and I saw so many fish and even a sea turtle!! We stopped at Maya Bay and it was everything I hoped for and more. If you ever go to Thailand you HAVE to see the Phi Phi Islands, no excuses!!
